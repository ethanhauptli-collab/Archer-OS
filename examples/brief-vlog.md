What it is: a personal YouTube vlog episode about a family day trip.

Audience: parents and people who like candid, reflective family content.

Tone: warm, honest, a little funny. Keep the real, unscripted moments with the kids; cut the logistics talk (parking, "is this recording").

Structure: open cold on the strongest emotional or funny moment, then roughly chronological. End on a reflective line to camera if there is one.

Must keep: any moment where one of the kids says something unexpected.

B-roll: cover talking-to-camera jump cuts with scenery and kid moments. Prefer wide establishing shots at the start of each new location.

Music: light bed under the montage-y parts, not under long talking stretches.
